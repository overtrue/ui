# 示例插图与项目封面

使用内置 imagegen 生成。插图采用钴蓝、白纸与银色材质，并保留透明背景；项目封面采用同色系的编辑摄影风格。生成后仅做 WebP 编码和等比例缩小，未改变图像内容。

资源位于 `public/assets/overtrue/`。插图宽度 1024px，WebP quality 88、alpha quality 100；封面宽度 1280px，WebP quality 86。其余场景摄影与头像沿用原素材。

## 提示词

### illustration-start.webp

无参考图。

透明背景：是。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: transparent editorial illustration for a polished modern software UI, "A fresh start".
Create a single beautifully crafted cobalt-blue folded paper plane, suspended at a gentle upward angle, with one softly curved ivory paper ribbon below suggesting lift. A restrained sculptural still life, not a cartoon icon. Crisp folds, tactile thick paper, subtle satin reflections on the blue surface, delicate ambient occlusion. Palette: pure cobalt #2a5bff, porcelain white, cool silver grey only. Three-quarter view with an elegant asymmetric diagonal composition; the subject takes about 75% of a landscape 4:3 canvas and remains legible at 240 pixels wide. Soft large studio light from upper left, refined realistic materials, clear silhouette. Transparent background with true alpha; no white rectangular backdrop, no floor or pedestal, no frame, no UI panels, no text, no letters, no watermark, no stars or confetti. Avoid beige, muddy greens, purple gradients, childish clay, inflated toy shapes, excessive gloss. High-end editorial art direction, meticulous and understated.
```
